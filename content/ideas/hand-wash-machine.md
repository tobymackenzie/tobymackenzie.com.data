Hand washing machine
======

Horizontal hole that you stick your hands into.  Three stages: clear, rinse, dry.

- wash phase would be pressurized soap, water, and grit.
	- grit would be natural such as sand, poppy seeds, oat husks
	- heated internally
	- recycling of water and grit until it reaches a certain level of dirtiness
	- high pressure like massage shower
	- 15 seconds or whatever it takes until dirt stops coming off hands, via sensors
	- rub hands together
- rinse stage would be high pressure water
- dry stage would be high pressure warmed air
