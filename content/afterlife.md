If there is an "afterlife", I would think it is most likely some form of reincarnation.  As a scientific basis, I might posit that some bit of our "soul" is in each of our atoms or smaller, and each of these will continue to exist going forward, possibly becoming part of another life form.  As such, our mind would be lost, and ourselves likely spread out, but still the bits of our soul would be there to make their influence toward better.

Alternatives
--------

However, I have thought of various afterlife possibilities that I think would be cool or interesting, and will share some of them here.

### Exploring the universe

We could be allowed to observe all time and space in the universe in order to understand everything, especially its relation to our life.  We would float around like an invisible drone with vision and other senses, able to move in any direction, forward or backward in time, travelling at whatever speed we want.  The amount of information out there being infinite, so would our task be of understanding it.

### Exploring our life

We could be given forever or thereabouts to go to any and all times in our life and observe and experience them until we fully understand them.  We could enjoy the good parts and accept the bad parts.  In the interest of understanding, we might also be able to observe any activities of people or things that are connected to our lives.  We could better understand why people made choices and reacted to us in the way they did.

### Exploring our life's affects

Similar to above but just the other people and things part, we could be shown the experiences of the others in relation to how we have affected them.  We would do this to understand our life's affect on others individually and broadly.

A variant of this, more in line with heaven and hell, would be that we could be forced to feel the effects of our actions on others in a guided rather than exploratory fashion.  The more good affects we had, the more like heaven it would be.  Conversely, the more bad affects, the more like hell.

### Following people we know

We could be given the ability to follow and invisibly observe people we knew in life, in order to understand our life's affects and possibly have some small bit of influence on them and things around them to improve their lives or guide them in the right direction.  We would do so in real time.

### Continuing on as presence

We could be an invisible presence that can move, observe, and possibly influence things after our death.  Like above, be we'd be able to observe and try to affect things in real time.  We'd exist in a point in space and be able to move about as we please.

### Retry our life

We could relive our own life over and over again, possibly with some level of reflection in between with memory of all previous lives, and some level of innate understanding carried on to our next go around.  We would try to improve things from previous attempts, set right any wrongs, etc.  It's possible we would do this until we hit a certain level of perfection, or it could just continue forever.

### Discuss with others

We could be given the chance to meet with and talk with people we met in life, and possibly any other dead people, as disembodied entities.  We would be able to discuss how we affected each other, how the world was or is going, or anything else we want, like just cracking jokes or telling stories.  We might do this just for understanding or experience.

We might be able to observe the world as it continues after our death and discuss it.  We might even be able to collectively have a small amount of influence on it.
