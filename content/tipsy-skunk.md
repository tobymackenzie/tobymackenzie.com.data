The Tipsy Skunk
=========

I once had plans to start a restaurant with my brother.  We were going to call it The Tipsy Skunk.  It was going to have a variety of foods including:

- spaghetti where you choose a pasta, a sauce, and a protein
- Jello Juice, ie the liquid you get making Jello without cooling it
- Soup from the Tire, a variety of soups that might also be done as delivery

We talked about plans for it a fair amount.  When I realized that I didn't want to stay in restaurants and go into management, though, that dream kinda died.
