Happy Thanksgiving once again.  This year's get together was a pleasant, simple four person affair.<!--more-->  Most of the extended family were off with the other side of their families or elsewhere.  It went very well and was less stressful than the big ones can be.

We had turkey, stuffing / dressing, mashed potatoes, gravy, corn, Brussels sprouts, cornbread, and Hawaiian rolls for dinner.  All very delicious.  I ate a lot.  For dessert, apple pie from my brother and pecan blondies from me ([Martha Stewart's recipe](https://www.marthastewart.com/1528873/butter-pecan-blondies), with slight modifications like no rum).  We all thought they were tasty, so I will probably try them again.

We watched parts of a couple of football games.  The first was interesting as football games go, but I don't really like football that much.  We watched the news and some other things as well, as we hung out there for a while.

During the day, I also took two rainy walks.  My coat (down) and shoes and socks got fairly soaked both times.  My pants got pretty wet, especially at the bottoms, the first time, so I wore rain pants the second.  The first walk was on the towpath at Beaver Marsh.  Saw ducks and geese on the water, and 4 or 5 other people on the trail.  Second walk was just up my parents' street, but I saw two deer there.

'Twas a good day.  I am thankful for the good day with family, daily walks, and generally things going well in life aside from a few issues here and there.
