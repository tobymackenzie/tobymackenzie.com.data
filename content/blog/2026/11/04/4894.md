I, like many US folk, voted yesterday.<!--more-->  I walked to my voting location as usual.  It ended up being the longest wait I've had at this location, and probably any in a while.  That may have been partly due to technical difficulties for the staff though.  Thus, I was standing quite a while, which made me feel a bit worn by the end of it, which led me to walk to a restaurant to get some lunch and refuel before the walk home.

The technical difficulties seemed to be two-fold.  They had three intake people, but only one was moving through voters for a time.  One seemed to be having difficulty logging into his tablet, and would ask the person next to them for help between voters.  And another was focused on a voter who must've been deregistered for some reason.  Thus, there was a line down the hall for them, but plenty of the voting booths left open.  Most of the time when I go to this voting location, the line doesn't even make it out to the hall if there is any line of note.

It was worth it though.  It seems the results were more to my liking than in recent elections.  Quite strongly Democrat, which, if nothing else, should help restore balance to the government.  I was, in fact, quite surprised at how strongly Democrat it was.  Some winning in places they haven't in decades.  I was a bit worried with gerrymandering, voter purges, threatened law enforcement involvement at election facilities, lawsuits, etc by those in power that it would be a hard road for the opposition.

I feel more positive about the next couple years.
