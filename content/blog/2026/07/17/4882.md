It was quite smoky today.  Lots of smoke wafted down from Canadian wildfires to Akron, Ohio and beyond.<!--more-->  It was visibly hazy and made the day fairly gray even though it was a "sunny" day.  The smell was strong, like a campground with lots of people burning fires and no wind to carry the smoke away.  It bothered my eyes and lungs, and I replaced part of my walks with the exercycle.  I didn't do some gardening I had hoped to get to.  Hopefully wind or rain comes soon to knock this out.  And rain to knock out those fires too.
